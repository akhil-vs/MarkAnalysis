# Outreach email — school principal

Copy, personalise the bracketed fields, and send **before** the meeting. A shorter thank-you variant sits at the bottom for the same-day follow-up.

---

## Subject line options

1. `Request for a short meeting — marks & results workflow for [School Name]`
2. `Reducing exam-week consolidation load at [School Name]`
3. `Pilot conversation: School Marks Analytics for your next unit / mid-term`

**Recommended:** option 1 for cold outreach; option 2 if you already know their pain; option 3 if a soft introduction already happened.

---

## Email draft (request a meeting)

```text
Subject: Request for a short meeting — marks & results workflow for [School Name]

Dear Principal [Last Name],

I hope this note finds you well. I am writing from PencilLabs regarding
School Marks Analytics — a role-based platform that helps principals and
exam co-ordinators run the full exam cycle without scattered spreadsheets.

In most schools we speak with, the week after an exam still looks familiar:
teachers keep personal mark sheets, the office merges files under deadline
pressure, and it is hard to know — with certainty — whether a consolidated
list is complete before it reaches parents or the board.

School Marks Analytics addresses that with one controlled workflow:

  • Teachers enter marks as drafts and submit their registers
  • You (or your exam co-ordinator) approve before anything becomes official
  • Analytics, consolidated mark lists, hall tickets, and parent-facing
    outputs all draw from the same approved data
  • Your school name, address, and crest print as letterhead on every download
  • Staff join with a school code that only you control

I would value 30–45 minutes with you (and, if convenient, your exam
co-ordinator) to walk through a live demonstration and discuss whether a
short pilot on one exam cycle would be useful for [School Name].

Would any of the following times work for a brief meeting — on campus or
by video?

  • [Day, date] at [time]
  • [Day, date] at [time]
  • [Day, date] at [time]

If none of these suit, I am happy to follow your calendar. I have attached a short leave-behind summary and can share the
PowerPoint briefing (School Marks Analytics — Principal Pitch) ahead of
our conversation if helpful.

Thank you for your time and for the work you do for your students.

With regards,

[Your Full Name]
[Title], PencilLabs
[Phone]  ·  [Email]
[Meeting link or office address, if relevant]
www.pencillabs.space
```

## Email draft (same-day thank-you after the meeting)

```text
Subject: Thank you — next steps for [School Name] pilot

Dear Principal [Last Name],

Thank you for making time today to discuss School Marks Analytics with
[me / us]. It was helpful to understand how [School Name] currently moves
marks from teachers to consolidated lists, and where the pressure builds
during exam week.

As discussed, the proposed next step is a 2–4 week pilot on
[exam name / unit / mid-term] for [classes / sections], with:

  • [Co-ordinator name] as the operations champion
  • [Teacher name] entering marks for the pilot papers
  • Setup support from our side in the first week

I am attaching:

  1. The PowerPoint briefing (School-Marks-Analytics-Principal-Pitch.pptx)
  2. The one-page leave-behind
  3. A short pilot checklist for your confirmation

If the scope above is correct, kindly reply with a preferred start date
and we will schedule a 30-minute setup call with your co-ordinator.

Please do not hesitate to share any sample mark list or board format you
would like us to mirror during the pilot.

With appreciation,

[Your Full Name]
[Title], PencilLabs
[Phone]  ·  [Email]
```

## Tone checklist before you send

- [ ] School name spelled correctly; principal title and surname verified
- [ ] No pricing unless they already asked — keep this email about time and control
- [ ] No jargon (multi-tenant, MFA, stack) unless writing to IT
- [ ] Offer two or three concrete meeting slots
- [ ] Attach only the one-pager for cold outreach; send the full deck after the meeting
- [ ] Sign off with a real phone number someone will answer
