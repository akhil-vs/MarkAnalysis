## A. Cold sequence — bumps & break-up

Use after the meeting-request email in `principal-outreach-email.md` if there is no reply.

### A1 — Bump (Day 3)

**Subject options**

1. `Re: marks & results workflow for [School Name]`
2. `Quick follow-up — [School Name] exam consolidation`

```text
Dear Principal [Last Name],

Following my note earlier this week: I am still keen to show how
School Marks Analytics keeps draft registers separate from official
consolidated lists — so nothing reaches parents until you approve.

Would [Day, date] at [time] or [Day, date] at [time] work for a short
video or on-campus walkthrough with you and your exam co-ordinator?

If email is inconvenient, a one-line reply with a better contact is enough.

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```

**Attach:** none (thread already has the leave-behind), or re-attach leave-behind only if first send bounced attachments.

## B. Inbound `/request-pilot` acknowledgement

Send within **4 business hours** of a platform pilot request.

**Subject:** `We received your pilot request — [School Name]`

```text
Dear [Name / Principal Last Name],

Thank you for requesting a School Marks Analytics pilot for [School Name].
We review each request personally (invite-led — not open self-serve signup).

To match the right pilot shape, could you confirm:

  1. Which exam or assessment you want to run first?
  2. Which classes / sections should be in scope (keep it small for 2–4 weeks)?
  3. Who will be the day-to-day champion (usually the exam co-ordinator)?

Once we have that, we will either schedule a short demo or provision your
private campus and send sign-in details.

Privacy notes for operators: [Product URL]/privacy

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```

## C. Pilot activation nudges

Use after the “campus is ready” email in `principal-outreach-email.md`.

### C1 — Day 3 activation

**Subject:** `[School Name] pilot — crest, exam, and first teachers`

```text
Dear Principal [Last Name],

A short check-in on your School Marks Analytics pilot.

By the end of this week it helps if:

  • School profile shows the correct display name, address, and crest
  • The pilot exam and class set exist under Records
  • Your co-ordinator and pilot teachers have requested access with the
    join code and been activated

If anything is blocking setup, reply with a screenshot or a time for a
15-minute call and we will unblock it.

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```

### C2 — Day 10 mid-pilot

**Subject:** `[School Name] — path to an official consolidated list`

```text
Dear Principal [Last Name] (cc [Co-ordinator name]),

Halfway through the pilot window, the useful checkpoint is:

  Teachers enter → submit → you approve → Consolidated lists shows Ready
  → official PDF/Excel (not a preview)

Pending uploads and teacher notices are there so incomplete papers surface
early. Role manuals remain under HELP → User manuals after sign-in.

When your first division is fully approved, try an official download once —
that is the pilot success bar we agreed.

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```

### C3 — Pilot close / success ask

**Subject:** `[School Name] pilot — did the official CML land cleanly?`

```text
Dear Principal [Last Name],

As we close the [exam name] pilot window: were you able to produce an
official consolidated mark list without merging spreadsheets?

If yes, I would value one sentence on what changed for your co-ordinator
(with your permission we may reuse it only in private follow-ups — never
publically without written OK).

If anything still felt awkward, tell us plainly — we will fix process or
product before you consider a wider roll-out.

Suggested next step: run [next exam / adjacent grades] on the same campus
while the workflow is fresh.

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```

## E. Soft nurture (Tier C / parked)

```text
know every paper is in before the consolidated list goes out?

exactly that control (draft → approve → official downloads). Happy to
show it in 30 minutes, or you can note interest at
[Product URL]/request-pilot.

With regards,
[Your Full Name] · PencilLabs · [Phone]
```
