# Parent / student portal — quick guide

The parent/student portal is a **read-only** view of **approved** marks for students linked by school leadership.

## How access works

1. A principal or exam coordinator creates a portal link for one or more students (and optionally one exam).
2. The guardian opens `/portal` and pastes the token / follows the issued URL.
3. The portal session lasts up to **12 hours**. After that, open the link again.
4. Leadership can revoke a link at any time.

## What you see

- Approved marks only (draft / submitted-but-unapproved registers stay hidden)
- Subject scores, grades, and related summary for the linked exam(s)

## What you cannot do

- Change marks, request edits, or message teachers through this portal
- See students who were not included on the link

## Privacy

Treat the portal link like a password. Do not post it publicly. See [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) for operator obligations.
